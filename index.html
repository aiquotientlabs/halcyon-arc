Why one company does both

Healthcare AI is only as good as its safety and security.

The people who most need AI's help are often the most vulnerable to its failures. A memory-care companion that can be manipulated, or a screening model that leaks voice recordings, causes real harm. So we build our brain-health products on the same safety, security and cyber disciplines we offer to clients, and we use those products as our own toughest proving ground.

  • Every model is evaluated for safety and bias before release, and monitored after.
  • Every AI feature is threat-modelled against prompt injection and data exfiltration.
  • Every environment is hardened, logged and continuously assessed.

Our principles

Designed to be calm under pressure

Safety before scale

We release only what we can evaluate, explain and roll back. Red-teaming is part of shipping, not an afterthought.

Canadian by default

Data is hosted in Canadian cloud regions and handled in line with PIPEDA and provincial health-privacy law such as Ontario's PHIPA.

Humans stay in charge

Our AI informs clinicians, caregivers and security teams. Consequential decisions remain with people.
