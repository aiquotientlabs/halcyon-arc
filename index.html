Our direction
Our guardians

Two platforms stand between our AI and the people it serves.

We built the AI Safety and AI Governance platforms for ourselves, because no off-the-shelf tool met the bar that health care demands. Every Halcyon Arc product has to pass through both.

Guardian one

Halcyon Arc AI Safety Platform

Clinical evaluation suites, red-teaming for vulnerable users, fairness testing across accents, languages and ages, and runtime guardrails that screen every live conversation for unsafe advice, prompt injection and personal-data leaks.

Guardian two

Halcyon Arc AI Governance Platform

A registry of every model we run, risk and impact assessments, release gates with clinical sign-off, a consent and data-lineage ledger, immutable audit trails and an incident process, so every AI decision is accountable to a person.

How the guardians work together

Nothing reaches a patient or caregiver without passing both.

The people who most need AI's help are often the most vulnerable to its failures. A memory-care companion that can be confused or manipulated, or a screening model that leaks voice recordings, causes real harm. So safety and governance are not features we add at the end. They are the gates every release passes through.

  • AI Safety evaluates every model before release and screens every interaction after it.
  • AI Governance records who approved each release, on what evidence and under which consent.
  • Clinicians and caregivers stay in charge of every consequential decision.

Our principles

Calm, careful and accountable

Safety before scale

We release only what our AI Safety Platform can evaluate, explain and roll back. Red-teaming is part of shipping, not an afterthought.

Canadian by default

Health data is hosted in Canadian cloud regions and handled in line with PIPEDA and provincial health-privacy law such as Ontario's PHIPA.

People stay in charge

Our AI informs clinicians, caregivers and families. Consequential decisions about health remain with people.
