About Halcyon Arc

We build the calm after the storm of new technology.

A halcyon is a kingfisher, and in old legend the calm seas it brought. The arc is the long curve of a life and of a system's behaviour over time. Our name is our promise: steady, trustworthy technology across the whole arc.

Our story

Healthcare first, guardians always

Halcyon Arc was founded in 2026 in Waterloo, Ontario, by a technologist who has spent years building voice, cloud and agentic AI systems. Two things kept coming up. Families facing dementia had remarkably few good tools, even as speech and language AI made new kinds of help possible. And there was no easy way to show that healthcare AI was safe and well governed enough to trust.

So we started with dementia detection and dementia care as our first undertakings, and built two guardian platforms, AI Safety and AI Governance, to hold every product we ship to the bar health care demands. Over time we plan to bring the same approach to everyday health questions and mental-health support.

Responsible AI

Commitments we hold ourselves to

Transparency

We document what our models do, how they were tested and where they fall short, and share that with customers.

Evaluation before release

No model reaches users without passing our AI Safety Platform, and none stays without monitoring.

Human oversight

Consequential decisions about health and care remain with qualified people.

Privacy & consent

Minimum data, clear consent, Canadian residency and no sale of personal information. Ever.

Dignity

Our healthcare products are co-designed with people living with dementia and their caregivers.

Accountability

Every AI system is registered in our AI Governance Platform with a named owner, an incident process and a channel for anyone to raise concerns.
