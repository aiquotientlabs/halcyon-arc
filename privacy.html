Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who we are

Halcyon Arc Inc. ("Halcyon Arc", "we") is located in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains how we handle personal information through our website and services, in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial laws, including Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) where it applies.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you send us, such as name, work email, organization and message.
  • Website data such as basic, privacy-respecting analytics about pages visited. We do not use advertising trackers.
  • Service data our customers or participants provide when using our products, governed by a separate agreement or consent form.
  • Health information only within our brain-health products and research studies, with explicit, informed consent.

How we use it

To respond to enquiries, provide and improve our services, secure our systems, meet legal obligations and, where you consent, conduct research. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use customer or participant data to train general-purpose AI models without explicit consent.

Where data is stored

We use Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud. Personal and health information for our products is stored in Canadian data-centre regions. Some service providers may process limited information outside Canada; where they do, we use contractual and technical safeguards.

Security

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, multi-factor authentication, logging and regular security assessments.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal limits. Contact our privacy officer at privacy@halcyonarc.ai. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
