Halcyon Arc for AI Safety

Know how your AI behaves before your users find out.

Evaluation, red-teaming and governance for large language models and AI agents. Halcyon Arc for AI Safety gives product, risk and compliance teams evidence they can stand behind.

The challenge

Models change weekly. Assurance usually doesn't keep up.

Teams ship AI features faster than they can test them. Hallucinations, harmful outputs, bias and over-confident agents surface in production, where they cost trust, money and sometimes safety. Regulators and enterprise buyers increasingly expect documented evidence of how a system was tested.

  • Repeatable evaluation suites tailored to your domain
  • Adversarial red-teaming by people and automated attackers
  • Governance artefacts your auditors and customers can read

Capabilities

Everything you need to assure an AI system

Domain evaluation suites

Test sets built from your real use cases, covering accuracy, groundedness, refusal behaviour and tone, scored automatically and by expert reviewers.

Red-teaming

Structured adversarial testing for jailbreaks, unsafe advice, manipulation and misuse, combining human specialists with automated attack generation.

Bias & fairness analysis

Measure differences in outcomes across demographic and linguistic groups, including English and French Canadian users.

Agent behaviour testing

Simulate multi-step tasks to check that agents stay within scope, ask for confirmation and fail safely.

Production monitoring

Continuous sampling and scoring of live traffic, with drift alerts when model updates change behaviour.

Governance & documentation

Model cards, risk registers and impact assessments mapped to recognized frameworks.

How it works

From first assessment to continuous assurance

Scope

We map your AI system, its users, data and the harms that matter most in your context.

Evaluate

We build and run an evaluation suite and a red-team campaign, then rank findings by severity.

Remediate

We help tune prompts, guardrails, retrieval and policies, and re-test until the findings close.

Assure

Evaluations run on every model or prompt change, with a living governance record.
