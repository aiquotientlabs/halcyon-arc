Healthcare product · Halcyon Arc for Dementia Care

A steady companion for every day, and everyone who helps.

Gentle routines and reminders for people living with dementia, peace of mind for family caregivers, and a shared picture for the care team.

See how it works
The challenge

Most dementia care happens at home, by people who are exhausted.

Family caregivers juggle medications, appointments, safety worries and their own jobs, often with little support. The person living with dementia wants independence and dignity. Care teams see only snapshots. We want technology that lowers the temperature, not one more app demanding attention.

  • Simple, voice-first help for the person living with dementia
  • Fewer worries and better coordination for caregivers
  • Clear, consented updates for clinicians and home-care staff

Capabilities

Support for the whole circle of care

Daily routines & reminders

Calm voice and visual prompts for medications, meals, hydration and appointments, adjusted to the person's pace.

Companion conversations

A patient conversational companion for reminiscence, orientation and gentle reassurance, with strict safety guardrails.

Caregiver alerts

Configurable alerts for missed routines or unusual patterns, sent to the people the family chooses.

Care-team coordination

Shared calendar, notes and handover summaries for family, home-care workers and clinicians.

Caregiver wellbeing

Check-ins, respite planning and links to local Alzheimer Society and community resources.

Consent & control

The person and their substitute decision-maker decide what is shared, with whom and for how long.

How it works

Set up together, in one conversation

Personalize

Family and the person living with dementia set routines, names, photos and preferences together.

Accompany

The companion offers reminders and conversation on a tablet, smart speaker or phone.

Coordinate

Caregivers see a simple timeline and receive only the alerts they asked for.

Review

Weekly summaries help families and clinicians adjust the care plan over time.

Safety first

Held to the standards of our own AI Safety and Security practice

The companion is red-teamed for manipulation, scams and unsafe advice, never gives medical instructions, and escalates to a human when something seems wrong.

Questions

Common questions

Is Halcyon Arc for Dementia Care available today?

It is in development. We are working with a small group of families and care organizations as design partners. Contact us to join the waitlist.

Does it replace a caregiver or home-care worker?

No. It is designed to support people, to reduce routine burden and improve coordination, not to replace human care.

Where is data stored?

In Canadian cloud regions, encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell personal information. See our privacy policy.

Does it provide medical advice?

No. The companion will not give medical instructions and directs medical questions to the person's care team or emergency services where appropriate.

Guarded by design

Protected by our AI Safety and AI Governance platforms

Every model in this product is evaluated and red-teamed by the AI Safety Platform before release, and screened by its runtime guardrails in use. The AI Governance Platform records each approval, the consent behind every piece of data and any incident, so clinicians and families can trust how it works.

  • AI Safety Platform: clinical evaluation, fairness testing and runtime guardrails
  • AI Governance Platform: model registry, consent ledger, release sign-off and audit
  • Health data hosted in Canada, never sold, and never used to train general-purpose models without explicit consent
