Healthcare product · Halcyon Arc for Dementia Detection

Notice cognitive change sooner, and more gently.

Short, at-home digital assessments that use speech, language and task performance to flag changes worth discussing with a clinician. Designed to widen access to screening, not to replace it.

See how it works
In development · Not a medical deviceHalcyon Arc for Dementia Detection is a research-stage product. It does not diagnose dementia or any other condition. We are seeking clinical and research partners to validate it, and we intend to follow Health Canada's guidance for software as a medical device before any clinical use.
The challenge

Many people live with cognitive decline for years before anyone checks.

Early signs are subtle, appointments are short and specialist waitlists are long, especially outside large cities. Yet earlier awareness gives families time to plan, address treatable causes and access support. Changes in how we speak, such as pauses, word-finding and sentence complexity, can be measured unobtrusively and repeatedly.

What we're building

Screening that fits into everyday life

Speech & language markers

Short recorded tasks analysed for acoustic and linguistic features associated with cognitive change, in English and French.

Digital cognitive tasks

Brief, game-like memory, attention and executive-function tasks that can be repeated without practice effects dominating.

Longitudinal trends

Each person is compared mainly with their own baseline, so gradual change is visible over months.

Clinician summary

A concise, explainable report a family physician can review, with the underlying measures shown, not just a score.

Privacy by design

Recordings are encrypted, stored in Canada and processed only with explicit, revocable consent.

Accessible by default

Large type, plain language, captions and caregiver-assisted mode for people with low vision or limited tech experience.

How it works

Ten minutes at home, a clearer conversation with the doctor

Consent

The participant, with family if they wish, reviews and gives informed consent in plain language.

Complete tasks

About ten minutes of speaking and memory activities on a tablet, phone or computer.

Analyse

Models extract measures and compare them with the person's own baseline and reference ranges.

Share

A summary goes to the participant and, if they choose, their clinician, with guidance on next steps.

Guarded by design

Protected by our AI Safety and AI Governance platforms

Every model in this product is evaluated and red-teamed by the AI Safety Platform before release, and screened by its runtime guardrails in use. The AI Governance Platform records each approval, the consent behind every piece of data and any incident, so clinicians and families can trust how it works.

  • AI Safety Platform: clinical evaluation, fairness testing and runtime guardrails
  • AI Governance Platform: model registry, consent ledger, release sign-off and audit
  • Health data hosted in Canada, never sold, and never used to train general-purpose models without explicit consent
